What JANSEVA does

Turns a photo into a complaint with the right category, location and office. Sends it by official email, portal links and optional X tags only after your tick, one complaint at a time. Then waits for you to say if it is really fixed. 17 languages, low data mode, works on slow phones.

How it works

1. Snap
Photo and GPS. Blur faces and plates on your phone.
2. AI checks
Category and severity suggested. You confirm.
3. Sent with consent
Body, state officer, ministry, MP. Up to 3 first emails, reminders at 7 and 15 days.
4. You verify
Authority says resolved is not enough. You confirm with an after photo.
