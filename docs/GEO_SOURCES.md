# Map boundary sources (problem-density map)
- Source: Local Government Directory (LGD) state and district polygons, 2024 snapshot, as published by bharatlas (https://bharatlas.com/view/lgd_districts and https://bharatlas.com/view/lgd_states), licence stated there: CC0-1.0 / CC-BY-4.0. Downloaded 2026-11-27 from https://data.bharatlas.com/admin/districts/LGD_Districts.geojson and https://data.bharatlas.com/admin/states/LGD_States.geojson. Attribution: "Boundaries: LGD (Govt. of India) via bharatlas, CC0/CC-BY, simplified".
- Why LGD: bharatlas states that its national boundary is dissolved from these LGD polygons "India-correct by construction (Aksai Chin via J&K/Ladakh, full Arunachal Pradesh claim)". India has laws on depicting its map (Geospatial Information Regulation Act 2016); this layer follows the Indian depiction. This is not legal advice: have someone check the final map before wide launch.
- Processing: mapshaper, Visvalingam 2% (states) and 1.5% (districts), keep-shapes, coordinates rounded to 0.02 degrees. Files: app/static/geo/india_states.geojson (36), india_districts.geojson (785). Simplified, so border points can fall in the neighbouring district; placement is approximate at borders.
- LGD districts are the 2024 set. Newly created districts or renamed districts after the snapshot are not in the file.
- Complaint counts are placed by the complaint's own coordinates (app/density.py). No tiles are used, so nothing external is loaded.
